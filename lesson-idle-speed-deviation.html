Idle Speed Deviation

Slide 1 of 3
Target vs Actual Idle

The PCM commands a target idle speed and then manages airflow to hold it. Idle speed deviation means the actual idle does not match the target, and codes like P0506 (lower than expected) or P0507 (higher than expected) can set.

Slide 2 of 3
High Idle vs Low Idle
  • Idle higher than target: think extra air, like a vacuum leak or a stuck open throttle or idle air control.
  • Idle lower than target: think restricted air, like a carboned throttle body or a dirty idle air passage, or a misfire or fuel problem.
Slide 3 of 3
Use the Scan Data
Compare desired idle to actual idle, and watch what the PCM is doing to try to correct it. Check fuel trim and loads such as A/C, which change the target.

Rule out leaks and carbon buildup before replacing parts.
