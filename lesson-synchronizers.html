What a Synchronizer Does

A synchronizer matches the speed of a gear to the speed of its shaft before the sleeve locks onto the gear's dog teeth. It uses a cone clutch and a blocker ring, so the shift happens smoothly instead of with a grind.

Slide 2 of 3
Worn Synchronizer Symptoms
  • Grinding when shifting into one particular gear, especially with a quick shift
  • The gear goes in when the shift is made slowly
  • Other gears shift normally
A worn synchronizer usually affects one gear, not all of them.
Slide 3 of 3
Grinding in Every Gear

If the transmission grinds going into most or all gears, suspect the clutch is not fully releasing. The input shaft keeps spinning, and no synchronizer can match speeds with it.

Check the clutch hydraulics or adjustment, and the disc and pressure plate, before blaming the synchronizers. Fluid type matters too, since the wrong fluid can change how synchronizers behave.
