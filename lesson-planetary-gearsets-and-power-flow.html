The Three Parts

A planetary gearset has a sun gear in the center, a ring gear on the outside, and a carrier that holds the planet pinion gears between them.

Every gear state comes from the same rule: one member is the input, one is held, and one is the output.

Slide 2 of 3
The Basic Rules
  • Reduction (low gear): drive the sun, hold the ring, output at the carrier. Torque goes up and speed goes down.
  • Overdrive: drive the carrier, hold the sun, output at the ring. Speed goes up.
  • Reverse: hold the carrier. The output turns the opposite direction.
  • Direct drive: lock any two members together and the whole set turns 1 to 1.
  • Neutral: nothing is held and nothing locks.
Slide 3 of 3
Working Out a Ratio

With the sun as input, the ring held, and the carrier as output, the ratio is (ring teeth + sun teeth) divided by sun teeth.

Example: ring 70, sun 30. (70 + 30) / 30 = 3.33 to 1. Always check the held member first when working a power flow.
