Lean Reading with High Positive Trim

Slide 1 of 3
The Pattern

The oxygen sensor reads lean (low voltage), and fuel trim is high and positive. Both are telling the same story: the engine is running lean, and the PCM is adding fuel to try to correct it.

Slide 2 of 3
What It Does and Does Not Mean
This pattern means the sensor is doing its job. It is reporting a real lean condition. It does not mean the sensor is bad.

The question to answer is why the engine is lean.

Slide 3 of 3
Where to Look
  • Vacuum or intake leaks letting in unmetered air
  • Low fuel pressure or a restricted injector
  • A MAF sensor that under-reports airflow
  • An exhaust leak ahead of the sensor, which can create a false lean reading

Check the trim at idle and at higher RPM to help narrow it down.
