What "System Too Lean" Means

A system too lean code (like the P0171/P0174 family) means the PCM is adding more fuel than normal, through fuel trim, trying to correct a mixture that keeps reading leaner than expected, and the correction has hit or is approaching its limit.

Slide 2 of 3
Where Extra Air Can Sneak In
  • A vacuum leak, a cracked hose, a bad intake gasket, letting unmetered air into the engine
  • A dirty or failing mass airflow sensor under-reporting actual airflow
  • Low fuel pressure or a restricted fuel filter, which isn't an air problem, but still results in a leaner-than-expected mixture since there's not enough fuel for the air that is present
Slide 3 of 3
Narrowing It Down
Check fuel trim numbers first. If both short-term and long-term trim are high across all cylinders, suspect a vacuum leak or airflow sensor issue. If the lean condition is isolated to one bank or a narrow RPM range, that points toward something more specific, like fuel delivery, rather than a widespread vacuum leak.
