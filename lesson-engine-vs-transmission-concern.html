Start with Data

Many complaints that feel like transmission problems are engine problems. Scan for codes, check freeze frame data, and check the basics first.

Slide 2 of 3
Sorting the Two
  • Misfires, rough idle, or low power in Park and Neutral point to the engine.
  • A shudder that matches engine misfire codes can feel like TCC shudder.
  • Engine rpm flares but vehicle speed does not follow: transmission slipping.
  • Problem only in certain gears or ranges: transmission.
Slide 3 of 3
Confirm Before Repair

Check fluid level and condition, compare engine rpm to turbine and output speed, and road test with a scan tool recording data.

Fix engine codes first if they could cause the symptom, then recheck the transmission complaint.
