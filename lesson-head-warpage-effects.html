Cylinder Head Warpage Effects

What a Warped Deck Does to the Casting

Cylinder head warpage isn't just a flatness problem at the gasket surface. The whole casting distorts slightly, and that distortion can carry into the valve seats.

A warped deck can pull the valve seats out of round, even though the seats themselves weren't directly damaged.
Slide 2 of 3
What a Warped Deck Does to the Gasket Seal

A head gasket needs even clamping pressure across a flat surface to seal correctly. A warped deck prevents that even contact.

Gaps between the gasket and an uneven surface let coolant or combustion gas find a path to leak, even with the head bolts torqued to spec.

Slide 3 of 3
Two Real Effects, Not One

Both outcomes (seat distortion and gasket leaks) come from the same root cause, an unflat deck surface, but they are separate consequences worth checking independently.

A resurfaced head corrects the flatness, which addresses the gasket sealing issue and removes the distortion that was pulling the seats out of round.
