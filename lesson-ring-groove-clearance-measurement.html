Ring Groove Clearance Measurement

Slide 1 of 3
What Ring Groove Side Clearance Is

Ring groove side clearance is the small gap between a piston ring and the top or bottom wall of its groove. That clearance allows the ring to move and seal correctly as it responds to combustion pressure.

Slide 2 of 3
How to Measure It
  • Fit a new ring into the cleaned groove, used only for measuring, not for final assembly.
  • Slide a feeler gauge in beside the ring, between the ring and the groove wall.
  • Compare the reading to the specification in service information.
Always measure against a new ring, not the old one. A worn old ring would hide how much the groove itself has worn.
Slide 3 of 3
Why It Matters

Too much clearance lets the ring rock or flutter in the groove during operation, which breaks the ring's seal against the cylinder wall and accelerates wear on both the ring and the groove.

Too little clearance can bind the ring, preventing it from seating properly against the bore.
